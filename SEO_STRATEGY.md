# SEO Strategy for Malek Fouda Portfolio

## Current SEO Issues & Solutions

### 1. **Google Search Visibility Issues**
**Problem**: Website not appearing in Google search results for "Malek Fouda" or related terms.

**Solutions Implemented**:
- ✅ **Structured Data**: Added JSON-LD schema markup for Person and WebSite
- ✅ **Meta Tags**: Comprehensive meta descriptions, keywords, and titles
- ✅ **Open Graph**: Social media sharing optimization
- ✅ **Robots.txt**: Proper crawling instructions for search engines
- ✅ **Sitemap.xml**: XML sitemap for search engine discovery
- ✅ **Security.txt**: Security contact information

### 2. **Technical SEO Improvements**
- ✅ **Canonical URLs**: Prevents duplicate content issues
- ✅ **Mobile-First Design**: Responsive design for mobile indexing
- ✅ **Page Speed**: Optimized loading times
- ✅ **Analytics Integration**: Google Analytics tracking implemented
